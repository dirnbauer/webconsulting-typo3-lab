# Suite Companion by webconsulting

Decision and sources checked on 7 November 2026. The user requested an
independent add-on for buyers of the official TYPO3 Suite, under Packages,
translated into English, German, Chinese and Hungarian.

## Offer

- Public webconsulting software remains free for everyone. Buying the official
  Suite is not a condition of access. No licence keys, private releases or paywall.
- The optional package is **annual Studio at €590 plus VAT**, with its existing
  scope: five live sites, one named developer, email answers within two business
  days and the existing maintenance promise. It is not a fifth pricing tier.
- Official Suite buyers requesting the package by **31 December 2026, 23:59
  Europe/Vienna** receive one **60-minute remote onboarding session per company**,
  to be held by 31 March 2027. Existing annual Studio customers qualify without
  paying twice. Verify the annual plan and Suite purchase before the session;
  request only a redacted purchase record if needed.
- The session reviews one project and agrees next steps for our tools.
  Implementation, migration and hosting require separate agreements.
- Official Suite licences, manufacturer responsibility and support remain with
  the original provider/partner. No official certification or endorsement is
  claimed. The independence notice appears before pricing on the overview and
  detail page.
- Enquiries go through the existing order explanation to the real
  [webconsulting contact form](https://www.webconsulting.at/kontakt).
  The demonstration lead form has example recipients and is not used for orders.

## Why this price and promotion

Free access fits the existing GPL model and removes a needless barrier. Making
personal support permanently free would introduce unbounded work. Charging an
extra software access fee would contradict the existing public release policy.
The established Studio price therefore offers a coherent starting point.

The timed bonus gives a specific reason to start this year without inventing a
discount, a former price or limited stock. It provides help with a first project
and does not create an annual entitlement to free workshops. At the internal
€130/hour service floor, the one-hour bonus accounts for €130 of capacity; €460
remains from the annual price before other support, overhead and acquisition
costs. This is a capacity check, not a profit forecast.

This is a recommendation based on the existing offer and cost floor, **not** a
validated willingness-to-pay optimum. Track qualified enquiries, accepted
offers, onboarding attendance and actual support hours. Review the economics
after the year-end campaign before extending it. Do not fabricate conversion
targets or customer proof.

Applied [Corey Haines' marketing skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills):
pricing and packaging around a clear value metric; copywriting with concrete
outcomes, one primary action, objection handling and honest urgency. Local
adaptations used: `typo3-agents/skills/pricing`, `copywriting` and
`marketing-skills/SKILL-PRICING.md`.
