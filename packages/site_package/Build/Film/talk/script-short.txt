Ten usability slogans every product team should know. Jakob Nielsen wrote them down in 1993, in his book Usability Engineering. They still hold.
One: Your best guess is not good enough. Test it.
Two: The user is always right about the experience.
Three: But not always about the solution.
Four: Users are not designers. Watch what they do.
Five: Designers are not users. They know too much.
Six: Vice presidents are not users either.
Seven: Less is more. Focused, not empty.
Eight: Details matter. Labels, errors, loading states.
Nine: Help doesn't. If users need help, fix the interface.
Ten: Usability engineering is a process. Research, prototype, test, repeat.
Usability is not magic. It is method.
