Nothing here reads the clock. No day is marked as today and no "open now" badge is drawn, because this page is cached: a badge rendered at three in the afternoon would still claim to be open at midnight, and a schedule that lies once is a schedule nobody trusts again. Exceptions belong in the note, where a human keeps them true. A closed day says the word as well as striking the hours through, so the state survives being read aloud.
