A seal names itself in writing under the artwork, which is what makes the strip readable to somebody who has never seen the mark before — and it is what turns each link into a distinguishable one. The artwork's alt is therefore empty: the name is already the next thing announced. Validity dates and certificate numbers go in the note, where an expired seal is easy to spot and remove; a seal that has quietly lapsed is worse than no seal at all.
