The list is maintained by hand, not generated from the page tree: a footer sitemap that scrapes the tree grows every hidden branch somebody forgot to exclude, and shrinks whenever an editor renames a section. An entry with an empty link opens a section, everything under it is a page in it, and the section headings are real h3 elements so that structure reaches a screen reader as headings rather than as a change of type size. Sections flow through the columns instead of each claiming one — with fifty entries a forced break leaves half the band empty. A heading may not be the last thing in a column, which is what break-after handles.
