One collection, sorted into two columns by the child's own toggle, so an editor moves an argument from one side to the other by flipping a switch rather than by cutting and pasting between two lists. The two markers are a plus and a minus, not two colours: on a monochrome printout and to anyone who cannot tell the two hues apart, the glyph is the only thing that still says which side a row is on.
