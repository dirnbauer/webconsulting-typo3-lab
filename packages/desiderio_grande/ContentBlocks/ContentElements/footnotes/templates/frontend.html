The marker is the note's position in the ordered list, not a number stored on the row: reordering the rows renumbers the list, which is exactly what an editor who inserts a source in the middle of an article needs to happen. Each item carries an id keyed to the record and the loop index, so a superscript in the prose above can link straight to it.
