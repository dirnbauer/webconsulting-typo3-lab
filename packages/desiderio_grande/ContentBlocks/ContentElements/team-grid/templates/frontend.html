Every portrait is cropped server-side to one ratio, so an upload of any proportion lands in the same box as its neighbours — mixed ratios are the fastest way to make a good team look untidy. The alt is the person's name rather than empty, even though the name is printed underneath: when a portrait fails to load, a card in a grid of faces should still say whose face is missing, and a reader moving through the page by image hears a roll call rather than a run of blank graphics. The child's own text is deliberately not rendered. A grid where everyone is the same size has room for a name and a role; one person writing three lines makes every other cell look empty. 'Leadership Row' is the element for people whose biography matters.
