Both column headings are h2, because the two passages are peers. Promoting one and demoting the other would tell a reader navigating by heading that the second column sits inside the first, which is the opposite of what a comparison says. Reverse swaps the columns with `order`, so the source keeps the left-hand text first and a one-column screen reads them in the stored order.
