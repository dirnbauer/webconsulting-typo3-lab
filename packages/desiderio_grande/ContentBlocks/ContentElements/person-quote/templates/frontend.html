The framing line sits above the quotation, never below: the reader has to know why this person is speaking before reading what they said, or the sentence arrives without a subject. Quotation, portrait and attribution are one figure, so the name is the caption of the quote rather than a paragraph that happens to follow it. The alt names the person. This is a colleague being introduced, not a decorative face beside a row of names, and the portrait is the only picture on the card.
