Plain links, not a switcher: nothing opens, nothing folds out, and the version you are already reading stays in the list rather than disappearing from it — a list that removes the current entry leaves a reader unsure whether they are on the right one. The entry with no link is the current version. It carries aria-current and says so in words as well, because a reader who cannot see the emphasis still has to know which one they are on.
