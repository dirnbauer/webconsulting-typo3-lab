Previous comes first in the source and on the page, because that is the order the two directions are read in. Either half may be empty — the ends of a series are exactly where this element earns its keep — and the half that is left then spans the band on its own. Each half is one link, and the page title is its accessible name; the word "Previous" or "Next" is a label above it rather than part of the link, so nobody hears "next" twice in a row.
