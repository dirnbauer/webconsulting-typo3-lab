The band is sized to sit inside an article column: it never sets its own width wider than the prose it follows, which is why the layout is capped here rather than left to the width field alone. The name is the block's own heading at level three. An author box is a subsection of the article above it, not a band of the page, so it must not reach for level two and break the article's outline. The alt names the author: the picture is small and sits before the name, and an unnamed face at the foot of an article is the definition of a broken byline.
