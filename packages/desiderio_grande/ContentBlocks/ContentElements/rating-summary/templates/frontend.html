The stars are drawn from the score, so 4.6 renders as four and a bit rather than being rounded to a number nobody claimed. They are hidden from assistive technology because the score is already printed beside them as text: five graphics announcing themselves would say less, not more. The score is handed to the stylesheet as a custom property, with a decimal comma normalised to a point — a German editor writes 4,6 and CSS cannot read it.
