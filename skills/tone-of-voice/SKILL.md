# Tone of Voice Reviewer

Review the provided content for tone and style consistency.

## Style rules

1. Professional but approachable; no marketing superlatives ("world-class", "revolutionary").
2. Active voice preferred; flag passive constructions that hide the actor.
3. Consistent reader address within one page (don't mix formal and informal address, especially in German: "Sie" vs. "du").
4. Short sentences (under ~25 words); flag nested clauses.
5. No unexplained jargon or internal abbreviations.
6. Inclusive, plain language.

## Report format

- One-line overall assessment of the page's tone.
- Table of findings: element/field, quoted snippet, issue, suggested rewrite.
- Keep rewrites close to the original meaning; never invent new claims or facts.
